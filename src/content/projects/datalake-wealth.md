## Problem

Wealth platform services needed consistent, audited access to Mongo-backed data without duplicating query logic across teams.

## What I built

DataLake Wealth — a wrapper (reusable component) around MongoDB services for efficient data access on BNY’s Wealth Online platform.

## Status

Shipped as a platform component used by multiple consumer banking features.
