## Problem

Indian consumers lose significant money to hidden charges, unfair bills, and opaque invoices. Human advocates are expensive; chatbots only summarize PDFs.

## What I'm building

AI Consumer Advocate — upload any bill or quotation. The agent explains charges, flags suspicious items, drafts complaints, and tracks resolution (with human approval before send).

First vertical on the OpenLifeOps platform. India-focused, multimodal, agentic.

## Status

Lab — MVP target: bill in → structured findings + negotiation draft out. Will deploy separately from this static site.
